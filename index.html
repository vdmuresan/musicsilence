The Story

The Original Idea

A glass sphere. A hall for nearly a thousand people, across nine levels: a fixed stage, and a slow, optional 360-degree audience platform — a kinetic mandala when the moment calls for it. The ceiling opens. It becomes a planetarium, or a silent room for yoga, meditation, drawing, and painting.

Reclining seats with deep lumbar support. A lobby open 24/7, wrapped in shelves of books, with free tea, coffee, water, and thoughtful snacks. The complex sits on 50+ acres of Florida Gulf Coast land: live oaks, meadows, a small lake, Japanese gardens, reflecting pools, forest bathing paths, and a golden dome with a roof garden.

This is the Temple of Music and Silence. A multi-purpose theater of music and the arts that also holds stillness. Built with the humility of nature. Cathedral-scale, yet intimate.

The Spaces

What the Temple Holds

01

Multipurpose Hall

One calm hall for concerts, silence, gatherings, yoga, and lectures.

02

Kinetic Stage

Optional rotating rings for special performances — a once-in-a-lifetime kinetic mandala. Musicians remain stable; the experience moves around them.

03

Silent Focus Spaces

Yoga, meditation, drawing, painting. The same architecture that holds a string orchestra can hold complete stillness.

04

The Living Lobby

Shelves of books on every level, open 24/7. Free tea, coffee, water, and snacks. A place to arrive, stay, and return.

05

Nature Sanctuary

50+ acres of live oaks, meadows, water, Japanese gardens, and forest bathing paths. The land is part of the temple.

06

Rooftop Garden

Terraced lawn, light canopy, a stage edge open to the sky. Weddings, cocktails, and concerts, lantern-lit at evening.

07

Beneath the Pond

Imagined: a rotating restaurant under the koi pond. Diners look up through the glass; from above, the room is visible in the water.
