The Vision

A multi-use temple and retreat space integrating concert halls, meditation rooms, gardens, water features, and nature. Designed for deep listening, movement, celebration, and stillness. Inspired by the provided architectural renders of domes, aquariums, zen gardens, and elevated concert venues.
