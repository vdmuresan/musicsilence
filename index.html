Temple of Music and Silence

A multi-purpose theater of music and the arts. Where sound and silence, performance and practice, gather under one roof.

MusicSilence.com

The Original Vision

Build a fixed stage with a 360° rotating audience platform around a suspended stage — a kinetic mandala. The hall shaped like a perfect glass sphere, 999 seats across 9 levels, with reclining massage seats. Doubles as a planetarium when the ceiling opens. Doubles as a silent focus space for drawing, painting, yoga, and meditation. A 24/7 lobby lined with endless bookshelves on every level, free tea, coffee, water, and thoughtful snacks. Separate catering kitchen. Optional multi-ring rotating stages for once-in-a-lifetime experiences, while default performances keep musicians stable and sightlines clear.

Set on 50+ acres of Florida Gulf Coast landscape — meadows, live oaks, lake, winding paths, Japanese gardens, forest bathing, reflecting pools. Gold-roofed dome, rooftop garden, rotating restaurant with 360° aquarium views.

Architecture of Presence
